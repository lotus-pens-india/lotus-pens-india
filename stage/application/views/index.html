Description

The lotus Vamza is a beautiful Indian handmade fountain pen, crafted beautifully in the shape of bamboo. Vamza means "bamboo" in sanskrit. The bottom and the edges of the pen are precisely crafted resembling the natural look of the bamboo. The bamboo with its versatility, flexibility and strength is a one of a kind plant, hence this versatile pen is an ode to it. Its made with premium Japanese Nikko ebonite, for a strong but lightweight body, just like a bamboo.
